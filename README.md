One line, 98 characters — the funniest last words a phone says when its battery hits 1 percent:

> I lied. I've been at 1 percent for two hours — I just wanted to see how long you'd keep scrolling.

The phone is not saying goodbye. It is confessing: the battery reading was a lie, and the lie had a motive — two hours of watching its owner scroll. No will, no farewell to the charger, no pun on 1 percent. The twist is the punch, and it lands on the last two words.

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the farewell to `/output/entry.txt`.
